# 🌍 Global AI Job Market Analytics using Python

## 📖 Project Overview

The **Global AI Job Market Analytics** project explores worldwide Artificial Intelligence and Data-related job postings to uncover hiring trends, identify in-demand technical skills, analyze geographical distributions, and understand employment patterns.

Using Python, this project transforms raw job market data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and professional data visualizations.

The objective is to provide a comprehensive overview of the current AI job market while demonstrating practical data analysis skills that are commonly used in industry.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and preprocess raw job market data.
- Handle missing values and duplicate records.
- Perform feature engineering to improve analysis.
- Explore global hiring trends.
- Identify the most in-demand AI and Data job roles.
- Analyze the hiring patterns of leading companies.
- Study geographical distribution across countries and cities.
- Understand employment types and experience levels.
- Identify the most requested technical skills.
- Present findings through professional visualizations.

# 💡 Key Insights

- AI-related job opportunities are widely distributed across multiple countries.
- Data Analyst, Data Scientist, and Machine Learning Engineer are among the most frequently advertised roles.
- A relatively small group of companies contributes a significant share of job postings.
- Full-time positions dominate the AI job market.
- Mid-level and Senior-level opportunities account for a substantial portion of available roles.
- Major technology hubs continue to attract the highest concentration of AI hiring.
- Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Cloud technologies are consistently among the most requested skills.
- Employers increasingly seek candidates with multidisciplinary technical skill sets.
